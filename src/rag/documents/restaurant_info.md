# FlavorCraft Bistro - Restaurant Information & Guidelines

## About Us
Welcome to **FlavorCraft Bistro**, where culinary passion meets fresh, artisanal ingredients. We specialize in handcrafted gourmet burgers, wood-fired artisanal pizzas, freshly rolled pastas, decadent desserts, and craft beverages.

## Ordering & Delivery Policy
- **Minimum Order**: No minimum order required for pickup; \$15 minimum for delivery.
- **Delivery Fee**: \$3.99 flat delivery fee on orders below \$40.00.
- **FREE Delivery**: Automatically applied on all orders totaling \$40.00 or more!
- **Estimated Delivery Time**: 30 to 45 minutes depending on traffic and distance.
- **Sales Tax**: 8.0% standard sales tax applied to all orders.

## Cancellation & Modification Policy
- Orders can be cancelled or modified within **5 minutes** of placing them, or while status is **CONFIRMED** or **PENDING**.
- Once an order enters **PREPARING** or **OUT FOR DELIVERY**, it cannot be cancelled or modified because food preparation is actively underway.

## Dietary Accommodations & Allergy Safety
- **Vegetarian Options**: Vegetarian items include Margherita Classica Pizza, Wild Mushroom Truffle Pizza, Truffle Parmesan Fries, Spicy Arrabbiata Penne.
- **100% Vegan Options**: Beyond Plant Power Burger (on gluten-free bun with vegan cheddar), Crispy Buffalo Cauliflower Bites, Vegan Garden Harvest Pizza, Vegan Chia Berry Parfait, Fresh Mint Limeade.
- **Gluten-Free Options**: Beyond Plant Power Burger, Vegan Garden Harvest Pizza (GF crust), Truffle Parmesan Fries, Buffalo Cauliflower Bites, Chia Berry Parfait.
- **Nut Allergies**: Our kitchen is mostly nut-free, EXCEPT for the *Cold Brew Hazelnut Latte* which contains roasted hazelnut extract. Please let the agent know if you have severe nut or seafood allergies.
